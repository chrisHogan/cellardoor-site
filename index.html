Cellar Door: Uncorked

Know what is in your wine fridge, shelf by shelf.

Cellar Door shows your wine fridge as it really is: each shelf, each row, each bottle where it sits. Photograph a label and the app suggests the producer, wine and vintage for you to check and save. Search the fridge, see what you have poured, and share the cellar with the people you live with.

You can also connect an AI assistant such as Claude or ChatGPT, so you can ask it what to open tonight.
