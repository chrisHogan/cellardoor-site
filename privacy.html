Privacy Policy

Cellar Door: Uncorked · Effective August 21, 2026

Cellar Door keeps your wine cellar on your phone. There is no account, no sign-up, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking. Most of what the app knows never leaves your device. This page describes the few cases where something does.

What stays on your device

Your inventory — wines, shelf placements, and poured history — is stored in a local file on your phone. If you configure a sync server, its address and API key are stored in the iOS Keychain. Deleting the app deletes all of this.

The camera

The app asks for camera access only when you use a scanning feature, and uses it only to read bottle barcodes and photograph wine labels. It never reads or writes your photo library, and label photos are not saved by the app — they are used once, as described below, and discarded.

Barcode lookups

When you scan a barcode, the app sends the barcode number — nothing else — to two public product databases, Open Food Facts and UPCitemdb, to pre-fill the wine's name for you to confirm. Neither service receives anything that identifies you beyond the ordinary technical details of a web request. You can turn these lookups off in Settings.

Label photos

If you use label-photo identification, the photo (and optional back-label photo) is sent over an encrypted connection to the app's wine-identification server, which passes it to Anthropic's Claude API to read the label and return the producer, name, and vintage as a suggestion. The server does not store or log your photos. Anthropic processes the photo to generate the suggestion and handles it under its own privacy policy; we use Anthropic's standard API service, and photos are not used to train its models. Nothing is added to your cellar until you review and save it.

Optional sync

If you configure a sync server in Settings, your inventory is backed up to that server so it can be restored later. Like any web server, it keeps ordinary technical logs (such as connection IP addresses) for a short time. If you never configure a server, your inventory never leaves your phone.

What we never collect

  • No accounts, names, email addresses, or phone numbers
  • No location
  • No contacts or photo library access
  • No advertising identifiers, analytics, or tracking of any kind

Retention and deletion

Data on your device is yours; deleting the app removes it. To ask about or request deletion of anything held on a sync server, email chris@hogan.org.

Children

Cellar Door is about wine and is not directed at children. It carries an 18+ age rating on the App Store.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
