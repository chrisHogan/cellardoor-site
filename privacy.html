Privacy Policy

Cellar Door: Uncorked · Effective November 21, 2026

Cellar Door is an iPhone app made by Chris Hogan, an independent developer ("we" below). It is built to keep as little about you as it can. There is no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and nothing is ever sold. This page lists everything the app and its server keep, and why.

Without an account

You can use Cellar Door without signing in. Your cellar then stays on your phone, and deleting the app deletes it.

Signing in with Apple

Signing in with Apple backs your cellar up, lets you identify labels with AI, share your cellar and connect AI assistants. For your account the server keeps:

  • the user id Apple gives this app (Apple's own pseudonymous id, not your Apple ID email);
  • a token from Apple, stored encrypted, used only to sign you out of Cellar Door at Apple when you delete your account;
  • your phone's model name (such as iPhone), kept with each sign-in;
  • when you signed up and last signed in.

The server does not keep your name or email address. If Apple shares your email at sign-in, the app keeps it on your phone only, to show which account you are using.

Your cellar

When you are signed in, the server keeps your cellar so it can be backed up, restored and shared: your wines and their details, where each bottle sits, what you have poured, your notes and tags, your fridge's layout, and a small thumbnail of each label you photograph.

Label photos and AI

When you photograph a label, the photo goes over an encrypted connection to our server, which sends it to Anthropic's Claude API to read the label. The suggestion comes back to you to check, and nothing is saved until you save it. Neither our server nor Anthropic keeps the full photo for us; Anthropic handles it under its own policy, and its API does not use it to train models. Drink-by estimates send a wine's details (such as producer, name, vintage and region) to Anthropic, never a photo. The server counts these AI calls for your scan allowance.

Shared cellars

If you invite someone to your cellar, the server keeps the invite's label and, once they join, their phone's model name (such as iPhone, or "Guest") and their role. A guest who links their Apple account is stored as a one-way hash of their Apple user id, not the id itself.

AI assistants

If you connect an AI assistant (such as Claude or ChatGPT), the server keeps the assistant's name and web address as it describes itself, what you allowed it to do, which cellar it can see, and when it was last used, linked to a one-way hash of your Apple user id. The assistant receives the cellar information it asks for and handles it under its own privacy policy. You can disconnect it at any time in Settings; its record is deleted 30 days after that.

Membership and purchases

Membership is bought through Apple. We never see your payment details. The server keeps the subscription's Apple transaction ids, product and dates, and Apple's notifications about it.

Server logs

Like any web server, ours writes request logs, which include your IP address and the address requested. They are kept by our hosting provider for a limited time and used only to run and secure the service.

Who processes data for us

  • Railway hosts the server and its database in the United States.
  • Anthropic reads label photos and estimates drink-by windows.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple and handles purchases.
  • Cloudflare runs this domain's DNS, and GitHub hosts this website.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Settings, Account, Delete account. That deletes your account, your cellar on the server, your signed-in devices and your assistant connections, and signs Cellar Door out of your Apple account. To stop a membership, cancel it in your Apple account's subscriptions as well.

After deletion the server keeps, with no expiry, so that deleting and re-creating an account does not reset what was used or undo a refund:

  • a one-way hash of your Apple user id, with the free scans and free drink-by estimates you had used, whether you had already been given or refused the one-time pool of extra scans a new membership comes with, the month of the deletion with the scans used in it, and the day of the deletion with that day's drink-by estimates and the AI calls charged that day without a result;
  • a one-way hash of each subscription that was given that one-time pool, or had been refunded or revoked, with whether the pool was given and, after a refund, when Apple signed it;
  • the date of the deletion and whether Apple confirmed the sign-out, with nothing that identifies you.

These hashes cannot be reversed, but anyone who already has your Apple user id or a subscription id could check it against them, so they are pseudonymous, not anonymous.

What we never collect

  • No location, contacts or photo library
  • No advertising identifiers, analytics or tracking of any kind
  • No selling or sharing of your information for advertising

Children

Cellar Door is about wine and is not meant for anyone under 18. It carries an 18+ age rating on the App Store.

Your choices

You can use the app without an account, turn AI features off in Settings, AI features, disconnect assistants and delete your account at any time in the app. To ask what we hold about you, or for anything else, email support@cellardooruncorked.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
