Support

Cellar Door: Uncorked

Questions, problems or ideas: email support@cellardooruncorked.com. Say what you were doing and what you expected to happen; a screenshot helps.

Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. Without one, your cellar stays on your phone. Sign in with Apple (Settings, Account) to back it up, identify labels with AI, share it, and connect AI assistants.

Why does the app ask for the camera?
To photograph wine labels and scan barcodes, so you do not have to type wines in. You can say no and enter everything by hand.

The label suggestion is wrong.
Every suggestion waits for you to check it, so fix any field before you save. A clearer photo of whichever label names the wine usually helps.

How do scans and membership work?
You get some free label scans to start. A membership, bought through Apple, adds more every month. To cancel, open your iPhone's Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. On a new phone, Settings, Membership, Restore purchases brings a membership back.

How do I share my cellar?
The owner of a cellar can invite someone from Settings, Sharing. They join with the invite in the app.

How do I connect an AI assistant?
In Settings, AI assistants, copy the address and add it to your assistant as a custom connector (in Claude: Settings, Connectors). Then sign in with Apple on the Cellar Door page it opens. You can disconnect it in the same place.

How do I delete my data?
Settings, Account, Delete account removes your account and your cellar from the server. Without an account, deleting the app removes everything. The privacy policy has the details.
