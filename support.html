Support

Cellar Door: Uncorked

Questions, problems, or suggestions — email chris@hogan.org and include what you were doing and what you expected to happen. Screenshots help.

Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. There is no sign-up and no login. The app works fully offline from first launch.

Why does the app ask for camera access?
Only to read bottle barcodes and photograph wine labels so you don't have to type them in. You can decline and enter everything by hand.

A barcode scan didn't find my wine.
Wine coverage in public barcode databases is patchy — this is normal. The scan still records the barcode, so after you enter the wine once, the next scan of the same bottle recognizes it instantly.

How do I delete my data?
Delete the app; everything it stores on your phone goes with it. If you configured a sync server and want that copy removed too, email chris@hogan.org.

What happens to label photos?
They are used once to read the label and are not saved. See the privacy policy for the details.
